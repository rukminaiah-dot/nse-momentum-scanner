Rule definition and current data coverage

Bottom reversal watch: P/E is shown as context only; one reading is not labeled cheap or expensive. Require fresh bullish Nifty structure plus at least two confirmations: India VIX at or above 25 and falling, DII cash buying absorbing same-date FII cash selling, or bullish RVOL at or above 1.5×.

Top reversal watch: fresh bearish Nifty structure plus at least two confirmations: India VIX up at least 5% versus its prior close, same-date FII cash net selling, or bearish RVOL at or above 1.5×. P/E alone is not a sell trigger.

This is a current-session rule check, not a trained or backtested model. P/E is context only; cash FII/DII is end-of-day; stale quotes and missing feeds stay unscored.

NIFTY 50 · Five-session event rules

NOT BACKTESTED
Event: at least +500 or −500 index points from the latest verified completed-session close within the next five trading sessions. The equivalent percentage and levels require that verified close.
How the research baseline decides

A countertrend setup is a reversal WATCH until NIFTY has moved at least 500 points from the verified anchor close against the prior trend; only then is it a confirmed 500-point reversal. The five-session period is the separate forecast horizon, not the movement threshold. Upside reversal watch: prior down regime, close above prior close and five-session average, plus 2 of 3 fresh confirmations: VIX at least 25 and falling, same-date DII buying absorbs FII/FPI selling at least 1×, or bullish RVOL at least 1.5×. Downside watch mirrors this after an up regime: close below prior close and five-session average, plus 2 of VIX up at least 5%, same-date FII/FPI net selling, or bearish RVOL at least 1.5×. Continuation requires aligned price/volume and two fresh confirmations; otherwise NO CLEAR EDGE.

Confirmations: timestamped VIX behavior, same-date FII/FPI-DII cash flows, verified relative volume/breadth, and time-stamped news or crude context. P/E is context only and needs a consistent historical earnings basis; it cannot trigger a call by itself. Missing inputs remain missing.

Outcome labels are UP ONLY, DOWN ONLY, BOTH thresholds touched, or NEITHER. If OHLC touches both thresholds in one session, their order is unknown. No probability appears until point-in-time walk-forward testing and an untouched final test pass calibration and error checks. This is not currently a trained ML forecast.

Medium-term reversal outlook · 60 sessions

RULES ONLY · NOT BACKTESTED

Suggested horizon: about three months. A sustained reversal label requires a 500-point move against the prior trend from a verified close, with at least 10 of the final 15 session closes beyond that barrier. Until observed, it is a watch only.

The 10-of-15 persistence rule is a provisional outcome definition, not a validated predictor. This app has not yet assembled the point-in-time historical inputs or tested forecast accuracy, so it shows no probability. A multi-year outlook would need a separate longer horizon.

Upstox global quotes are used as overnight context. No confidence percentage is invented.
